# Privacy Policy

**RandomReader** does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal data.

## What the extension does

- Fetches public RSS / Atom / sitemap feeds from the sources listed in its catalog.
- Stores fetched article links and your reading history **locally** in your browser using `chrome.storage.local`.
- Opens articles in your browser when you click **Surprise Me**.

## Data handling

| Data | Collected? | Where it lives |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Reading history | Yes (local only) | Your browser's local storage |
| Feed contents | Yes (local only) | Your browser's local storage |
| Personal data | No | — |
| Analytics / tracking | No | — |
| Third-party sharing | No | — |

All data stays on your device. Nothing is sent to any server operated by the extension authors. Clearing your browser data or uninstalling the extension removes everything stored.

## Changes

If this policy changes, an updated version will be published in this repository.
